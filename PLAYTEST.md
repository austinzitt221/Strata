# PLAYTEST — what to try and what I want to hear about

A running list, newest build first. Claude adds to it every build; Austin
plays through it in one go and reports back. Ticks and notes go in the
playtest report, not here.

## Build 26 — THE PALISADE
Craft them at the table (WEAPONS tab): claymores, one-way spikes, a
tripwire bell. Place them like any prop; they face the way you face.
- [ ] Claymore: set one at a cave mouth or on the path to your door and
      stand behind it at night. It should fire once for an enemy in its
      front fan, never for you, your crew or a sheep, and leave no hole.
      Is 70 damage at the muzzle (a husk has 95) right?
- [ ] Spikes: a strip across a doorway. Enemies should crawl across it
      bleeding; you should walk over it without noticing. Does it read
      as dangerous enough? Should it ever wear out?
- [ ] Bell: post an armed crew member on STAY 30 m from a bell, then
      lure something to the bell. Three notes, the message says which
      side, and the crew member should arrive and fight, then go back
      after half a minute.
- Feedback I want: which of the three would you build first, and which
  never? What is missing from a base's defenses that these do not
  cover (the roadmap still has the oil trench, portcullis, flame jet)?

## Build 25 — THE CREW (slice B)
- [ ] Stay out at night with a crew member who has no weapon. Enemies
      should go for whichever of you is nearer. Do they die too fast, too
      slow? (60 hp, a husk hit is 18.) When one dies their things should
      fall where they fell and the house should have a new name in it.
- [ ] GIVE a dispenser (the item, from a hotbar slot) and then a stack
      of material. Lay a wall while they follow: they should place the
      next shape along your aim in their material. Does the pace feel
      right? Do they place things you did not want?
- [ ] Save a blueprint (wrench select, Ctrl+C, N to save). In the crew
      panel PICK it and BUILD it where you look. Watch it go up. Is the
      facing what you expected (it faces the way you face)?
- [ ] Let them run out of material mid-build, then hand them more.
- [ ] Hurt crew members show a small bar over the head (green, amber,
      red) until they mend. Readable at a distance? In the way?
- Feedback I want: should a crew member flee when unarmed and hurt?

## Build 24 — THE CREW (slice A)
Find a village (the compass points at the nearest), trade with one
villager until goodwill reads 10, then look at the row under the
mission in their trade screen.
- [ ] HIRE for 60 coins. Does the row make sense before you can afford
      it (it greys out with the goodwill it needs)?
- [ ] Right click your crew member. Is the panel clear about what each
      order does? Is anything missing you expected to see?
- [ ] FOLLOW: walk, sprint, jump a gap, go down a cave, drive off in a
      car. They should never be lost for more than ten seconds; when
      they catch up by "any means" they appear beside you in a puff of
      cyan. Does that read as fine, or as a cheat you notice?
- [ ] GIVE them a drill (select it in the hotbar, click GIVE). Dig a
      tunnel while they follow: they should take the next cut along
      your aim and the tunnel should go about twice as fast. Do they
      get in your way? Do yields land in your pack (within 12 m)?
- [ ] QUARRY: look at open ground, click QUARRY. A 10 m pit, 6 m deep,
      top down. Walk away and come back: their pack should hold what
      they dug. COLLECT it.
- [ ] GIVE a sword or a gun, stay out after dark near your base. Do they
      fight what comes? Do they ever hit you? (They should not.)
- [ ] STAY and ROAM: park one on a hill with a gun, set ROAM to 40 m.
      Come back at night.
- [ ] Save, quit, load. Are they still yours, in the same place, with
      the same tool? Following ones should appear beside you.
- [ ] DISMISS: tool and pack come back to you; they walk home.
- Feedback I want: the price (60 coins, 10 goodwill) — too cheap, too
  dear? The follow distance (they stop 2.8 m off)? Their speed? And
  the lines they say: keep, cut, or write more of them?

## Build 23 — MIRROR
- [ ] Hold a drill, stand on the centre line of where a building will
      go, look down that line, tap K. A faint sheet should stand through
      the ghost, and the ghost should have a paler twin on the far side.
- [ ] Mine and place either side of it: every edit should land twice.
      Rotate a stretched cube (middle click cycles the axes) and place
      it: the twin should lean the opposite way, not the same way.
- [ ] Trowel, bore and lathe with the mirror on. A bore should give two
      tunnels; a lathe two towers.
- [ ] Shift + K turns the plane a quarter; K clears it. Does the sheet
      ever get in the way of seeing what you are doing? (I can dim it or
      shrink it.)
- [ ] Undo with the mirror on should take both halves.
- Feedback I want: is K the right key? Should the mirror also apply to
  the wrench's move/delete (it does not, on purpose)?

## Build 22 — MASS
- [ ] Trowel: scroll the disc up to 48 m. Hold left click on flat
      ground. A mountain in a few strokes; you should ride up with it,
      never get buried. Does the frame rate hold while it grows?
- [ ] Middle click swaps scroll between DISC and DEPTH. Try depth at
      15% (a thin skin of ground) and at 140% (a great blob).
- [ ] Shift + left click on a hillside: a flat plateau at the disc's
      height. Is the rim too soft, too hard?
- [ ] Lathe (6 iron ingots + a ruby ingot at the table): lay an axis
      point, then an outline out from it, right click. Then shift + right
      click with a smaller outline inside a tower to hollow it. Does the
      wireframe preview tell you what you will get?
- Feedback I want: 48 m — big enough? Anything the trowel should do it
  cannot?
