# PLAYTEST — what to try and what I want to hear about

A running list, newest build first. Claude adds to it every build; Austin
plays through it in one go and reports back. Ticks and notes go in the
playtest report, not here. Builds 21-29 were played on 2026-09-07 and
their lists are retired; what came of them is Build 30.

## Build 32 — THE CREW C
- [ ] Hire someone (village: 10 goodwill, 60c). Their panel has THEIR
      HANDS, three armor slots and an eight-slot PACK above your bags.
      Click a drill, a sword, a helmet, a stack of torches and a stack
      of stone in your bags: the drill goes to their hands, the helmet
      on, the rest into the pack. Right click one of yours to lift it
      and drop it into a slot of theirs yourself. Shift-click too.
- [ ] Take things back by lifting them from their slots. Swap the tool
      in their hands by dropping another on it.
- [ ] With a sword or gun in their pack and a drill in hand: let
      something come at night. The weapon should come out; when it is
      quiet again and you mine, the drill should come back.
- [ ] Give them twenty torches and walk them through a cave or a night:
      a torch should appear wherever it is dark, none by day, none next
      to one already burning.
- [ ] Armor on them: the tier colour on head, body, feet; the panel
      says the reduction.
- [ ] A city: hire a shopkeeper (120c) and, at standing 25, the mayor
      (400c). Their TRADES tab should still sell what the shop sold
      (the mayor's deed and blueprint market included). The shop door
      should stand empty.
- [ ] Fists down, left click a crew member: they ride over your head.
      Throw them somewhere. Same for a villager at 10 goodwill.
- [ ] Right click a tent at night: you sleep, and you wake at the tent.
- [ ] Dismiss someone carrying a full pack: everything comes to you.
- Feedback I want: is eight pack slots enough or mean? Should the
  weapon stay out at night even with nothing about? Does the torch
  spacing (seven metres) read as "lit" or as "a torch every few
  steps"? And the mayor at 400: cheap, dear, or about right?

## Build 31 — WATER RANGE
- [ ] Do the mountain thing again: cut a river open on a slope. The
      water should run out and down about thirty metres, then trail off
      and stop. Nothing should keep growing.
- [ ] Dig a pit in a lake bed: it should still fill.
- Feedback I want: is thirty metres of run-out too little to be fun, or
  about right? (It is one number.)

## Build 30 — PLAYTEST FIXES (from your report)
- [ ] Rivers up close: one continuous sloped sheet now, no stairs, no
      gaps. Look at the same stretch that stepped before.
- [ ] Walk up to a city building from outside: no LOD wall standing
      beside the real one at the edge of the near field. If it still
      happens, a screenshot with the compass readout in it helps.
- [ ] A new world: the tent at camp 1 is a tent; chests sit on the
      ground; camp 8's mouth has a stone rim and THE MOUTH marker
      appears when you reach camp 8. Loot ramps up camp by camp.
- [ ] Oil trench: lure something across it. It should die within
      seconds of leaving the oil. The tray vanishes when the fire is out.
- [ ] Spikes: after forty bites they bend flat. Fists pick them up.
- [ ] Crew: open their panel and click a drill or a stack of stone in
      your bags. It should go straight to them.
- [ ] Fists down: left click a torch, a table, a chest, a door, a bed.
      It comes back to you (a chest spills first).
- [ ] The burrower: dig down and meet it; it should never rise within
      eight metres of the surface. Sprint away for eight seconds and
      the ground should go quiet.
- Feedback I want: the trench's kill speed and the spikes' forty bites;
  whether "fists take anything" ever takes something you did not mean.
